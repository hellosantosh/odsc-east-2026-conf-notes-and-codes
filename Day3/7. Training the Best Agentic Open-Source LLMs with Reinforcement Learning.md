Reinforcement learning (RL) has become a practical and essential tool for training large language models (LLMs) that perform well in real-world applications. While large-scale pretraining establishes broad language capabilities, RL is increasingly crucial for improving instruction following, reasoning quality, and alignment with user and business objectives—making it a critical component of modern LLM production pipelines. This talk begins with a concise, practitioner-oriented overview of reinforcement learning for LLMs, including why approaches such as GRPO are used to train models for agentic system. We then examine the current open-source LLM landscape and discuss how leading open models achieve competitive performance through carefully engineered RL workflows. The core of the talk is a case study on training NVIDIA NeMotron models using NeMo RL, an open-source RL framework. We share concrete results showing how RL improves reasoning, agentic tool use, instruction-following accuracy, and alignment metrics, positioning NeMotron among the strongest open-source LLMs available today. In addition to results, we highlight practical lessons learned and best practices, including reward design, stability and scaling considerations, data curation strategies, and common pitfalls encountered when applying RL at scale. We also discuss infrastructure challenges we have encountered while doing large-scale RL and how they are resolved via NeMo RL. NeMo RL is built to integrate seamlessly with the entire Pytorch and Hugging Face ecosystem, enabling modular reward modeling and tool use, efficient distributed training, and reproducible experimentation on accelerated hardware. This open source toolkit is designed to help teams to try RL on a range of projects, from research prototypes to production-scale model training workflows. We conclude by discussing future directions for RL in open-source LLM development, including more efficient optimization techniques, richer feedback signals, and emerging trends that will shape how enterprises build, customize, and deploy high-quality open models.
