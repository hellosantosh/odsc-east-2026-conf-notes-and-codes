### How to Actually Scale Agentic AI in 2026: A Practitioner's View

#### Speakers: Andre Balleyguier, Applied AI Leader, Anthropic

Agentic AI is moving fast but most organizations are still tstuck on a handful of internal experiments. 

This talk daws on 10+ years of deploying AI/ML across enterprises to focus on what actually works: how to prioritize agentic use cases, design multi-step agent architectures, connect agents to enterprise systems through tool integraiton, and build the evualuation and observability layers needed to trust what your agents are doing at scale. It also covers the adoption patterns, human-in-the-loop design, and governance frameworks that separate organizations running one agent from those running hundreds.
