In this keynote, MIT professor Max Tegmark explores a fundamental shift in how we build software with AI - from today's "vibe coding", where models generate code that may contain hidden errors, to "vericoding", a new paradigm where AI produces code that is provably correct. Drawing on cutting-edge research in formal verification and AI safety, Tegmark will show how combining large language models with mathematical proof systems can enable software that is guaranteed to meet its specifications, not just tested for bugs. Attendees will gain insight into how this approach could transform software engineering, improve reliability in critical systems, and play a key role in building safe, trustworthy AI.
