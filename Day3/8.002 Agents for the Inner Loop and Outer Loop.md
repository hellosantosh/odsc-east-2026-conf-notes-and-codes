Most developers are now used to using agents as part of the inner loop of development. They run a CLI locally on their laptop, making a few changes at a time, and iterating until they’re satisfied with the code.But a growing number of developers are starting to use agents in the outer loop—to do code review, to fix CI/CD, even executing entire changes without ever opening their IDE.We’ll discuss when it’s appropriate to bring agents into the outer loop of development, and how—for certain tasks—they can deliver a huge productivity boost.
