Distinguished Professor of Computing at MIT Schwarzman College of Computing

Programming agents have made tremendous progress recently, to the point where many organizations can rely on them to produce most of their code. Despite this success, a number of open problems remain to push these tools to the next level of productivity. In this presentation, I will outline some of these open problems and provide an overview of some ongoing research aimed at addressing these limitations.
