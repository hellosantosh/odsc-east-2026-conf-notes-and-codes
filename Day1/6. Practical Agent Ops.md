Are your agents getting stuck in “PoC Prison”? Are you held up by limited evaluation sets and hardly any feedback? Are your stakeholders waiting around wondering when they’ll get to see the product demo? When you do get feedback, how quickly will it be incorporated? The latest major release of MLflow (version 3.0, released in summer 2025) was a major framework overhaul to help address these operational challenges and accelerate the path from proof-of-concept to production for agents and other generative AI applications. With its framework-agnostic APIs, enhanced tracing, dedicated tools for agent evaluation, and flexible deployment options, MLflow 3.0 makes it easier than ever to operationalize complex, multi-step agents. But where should you start? What are the most critical MLflow APIs to learn? What does the right development cycle look like? At Western Governors University, we’re using MLflow >= 3.0 to accelerate the deployment of our agentic applications—such as AI-assisted enrollment—and drive alignment between data science, engineering, and product teams. By incorporating MLflow earlier in the development pipeline, we’ve seen dramatic improvements: deployment cycles shortened from months to days, evaluation relevance increased tenfold, and cross-team feedback loops tightened to hours instead of weeks. In this interactive demo, we’ll tackle a real-world use case from start to finish. The session will teach you to: (1) set up an experimentation framework from scratch; (2) build critical evaluations with custom metrics for accuracy, safety, and relevance; (3) perform rapid A/B testing feedback iterations and debugging via MLflow tracing; (4) deploy versioned models to different production environments of choice. We’ll show you how to apply this framework to simple chat completions as well as more complex agents using popular open-source frameworks. We’ll zero in on the most essential components of MLflow 3.0 and demonstrate how they work together to accelerate the path to production all on your local machine. Join the workshop to learn how to quickly add powerful Agent Ops to your workflows, boost the quality and quantity of your evaluations (e.g., integrating human-in-the-loop reviews), and ultimately deliver enterprise value with reliable, high-quality agents. Some experience with Python is highly encouraged, as well as prior exposure to machine learning, LLMs, and agents.
