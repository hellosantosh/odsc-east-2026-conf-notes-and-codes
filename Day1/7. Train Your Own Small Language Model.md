Small Language Models (SLMs) are reshaping how engineers think about deployment tradeoffs, such as balancing performance, speed, and cost. In this hands-on workshop, we'll build a complete SLM pipeline from the ground up using open-source tools. You'll learn how to prepare your dataset, select and align tokenizers, configure architectures, and apply knowledge distillation to transfer insights from larger teacher models. We'll demonstrate how to monitor training runs, fine-tune hyperparameters, and apply quantization techniques to achieve efficient inference on consumer-grade hardware. This workshop isn't theory... it's the playbook for practitioners who actually want their models to work outside the lab. We'll dissect real-world pitfalls like tokenization mismatches, vanishing gradients, and memory constraints, showing how to debug and stabilize training runs. Attendees will leave with reproducible code, a working example model, and the confidence to train and deploy their own compact LMs for production workloads, experimentation, or homelab environments.
